SCIENTIFIC CONTRIBUTION
================================================================================

The documentation emphasizes a novel framing of molecular visualization:

"This visualization system embodies a fundamental principle of the 
scientific method: trust nothing that cannot be falsified... The system 
does not ask 'how can we make this data look convincing?' but rather 
'how can we make errors impossible to miss?'"

This positions ASVS as:
- Validation instrument, not presentation layer
- Falsification tool, not confirmation tool
- Epistemic tool, not rhetorical tool
